# Whiteout — Realism Standards

Whiteout should behave like an enterprise investigation, not a trivia puzzle.

## Evidence rules
1. Every scored conclusion must be recoverable from evidence.
2. High-impact conclusions require independent corroboration.
3. Benign noise must be plausible and relevant, not random clutter.
4. Missing telemetry must have a documented operational cause.
5. Timestamps must distinguish occurrence, collection and ingestion.
6. No question may depend on guessing an author's intention.
7. No real credentials, PII, customer data or functional malware.
8. Every released artifact must have SHA-256 and provenance.

## Operational realism
The environment must contain:
- genuine simulated communications degradation;
- legitimate emergency administrator actions;
- delayed centralized telemetry;
- host-local evidence that survives the visibility gap;
- backup/recovery complications;
- audit pressure;
- malicious actions whose classification requires correlation.

## Fairness
Difficulty comes from correlation and judgment, not hidden arbitrary facts. If two interpretations remain equally supported, the expected answer must permit uncertainty rather than force a false certainty.
