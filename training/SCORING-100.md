# Whiteout — 100 Point Operational Scoring

| Domain | Points |
|---|---:|
| Evidence integrity & provenance | 10 |
| Environmental timeline | 10 |
| Cyber timeline | 15 |
| Identity & authentication analysis | 10 |
| Cross-server correlation | 15 |
| Environmental/benign/malicious classification | 15 |
| Containment & continuity decisions | 10 |
| Final incident report | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Performance Bands
- **90–100 — Senior / Incident Lead Ready**
- **80–89 — Strong DFIR Analyst**
- **70–79 — Operationally Competent**
- **60–69 — Significant correlation/decision gaps**
- **Below 60 — Re-analysis required**

Correct guesses without evidence do not receive full credit. Unsupported containment decisions may lose operational-decision points even when individual artifacts were identified correctly.
